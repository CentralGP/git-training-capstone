# Request process

This document explains how the team handles a request from arrival to answer. Follow the steps in
order. The team reference lists the people, systems, forms and times.

## Steps

1. Requests arrive in the team inbox. The request owner triages each one.
2. Log every request in the shared drive so nothing is lost.
3. For an expense claim, the requester fills in Form PR-2.
4. For a purchase request, the requester fills in Form PR-2. It is answered within 5 business days.
5. For an access request, the requester fills in Form AC-3. It is answered within 2 business days.
6. Spending and purchases are approved by Lee Armstrong.
7. Expense claims are answered within 5 business days.
8. Store finished documents in the tracker.
9. The coordinator schedules the work and keeps the tracker up to date.

## Who to ask

- The request owner triages new requests.
- The coordinator schedules work.
